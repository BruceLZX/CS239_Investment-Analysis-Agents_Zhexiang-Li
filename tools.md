message_bot: Rakazo built-in, the Lead Analyst's only. Takes confirm_name (the recipient bot's name, e.g. "Market Analyst", "Red Team Analyst" or "IC Reviewer"), intent ("request") and message (the text to send, under 8,000 characters). Returns a receipt with ok, the recipient's bot id and name; the recipient's final reply arrives later as a new message to the sender.

web_search: Rakazo built-in (DuckDuckGo by default). Takes query and maxResults. Returns a list of results, each with title, url and snippet.

web_fetch: Rakazo built-in. Takes url and maxChars. Returns the page's url, title and text, cut to maxChars. Rakazo holds the fetch for approval when the URL did not come from the bot's own search results; filings are read with read_sec_filing instead.

find_sec_company: Takes ticker. Returns the company's ticker, 10-digit SEC CIK and registered name, from SEC EDGAR.

get_sec_financials: Takes ticker and years (1-5, default 3). Returns about 32 income statement, cash flow and balance sheet lines from the company's 10-K XBRL filings by fiscal year, with growth, margins, free cash flow, debt (naming the concepts it adds) and net cash worked out, and returns (ROE, ROA, an ROIC approximation), working-capital days and the cash conversion cycle, interest coverage, goodwill and intangibles as a share of assets, stock-based compensation as a share of revenue and capex against depreciation, each year and over the trailing twelve months with the formulas stated; and a table of the latest quarter, the same quarter a year earlier, year to date and trailing twelve months from its 10-Q filings. Registers the 10-Ks and the 10-Q it used as sources, credited to the caller, and lists their IDs and locations to cite.

list_sec_filings: Takes ticker, forms (default 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and DEF 14A) and limit (default 8). Returns the company's recent SEC filings, each with its form, filing date, report period, accession number and link.

read_sec_filing: Takes ticker, form (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K or DEF 14A; default 10-K), item (default 7), page (default 1), keywords (optional) and accession (optional, for a filing other than the latest). 10-K items: 1, 1A, 1B, 1C, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 7A, 8, 9A and 10-14 (which say when they are incorporated from the proxy statement), and EX-21, the subsidiary list. 10-Q items: 1, 2 and 1A. 8-K items 2.02, 5.02, 7.01, 8.01 and 9.01 are read from the latest 8-K that reports them, followed by its exhibit 99.1 press release; 99.1 is the release alone. DEF 14A sections: directors (with the executive officers), compensation, ownership, related, committees and auditor, found by their headings or, failing that, by keywords. Returns that item of the filing as text, about 9,000 characters a page and skipping the table of contents, or only the paragraphs that mention any keyword, searched on the MCP server, within the item or, with item empty, across the whole filing. Registers the filing as a source and lists its ID; returns an error listing the filing's items when the item is not found, and the valid items for a form.

get_price_history: Takes ticker and period (1mo, 3mo, 6mo, 1y, 2y or 5y; default 1y). Returns the exchange and currency, the latest price with its UTC timestamp, the low, high, first and last daily close over the period with the change in percent, the 52-week range, the change to the latest price over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and the market backdrop (S&P 500, VIX and 10-year Treasury yield), from Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint. Registers the price data as sources (medium confidence) and lists their IDs.

get_valuation_snapshot: Takes ticker and peers (up to 4 tickers). Returns, for the company and each peer, the price with its time, market cap, enterprise value and trailing-twelve-month P/E, EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield and P/B, worked out from SEC XBRL filings and Yahoo prices; a second table of EV/EBIT, P/FCF, dividend yield, buyback yield and total shareholder yield; the debt counted in each EV and the concepts it adds; the company's P/E and EV/Sales at its last five fiscal year ends; the peer median and the company's premium or discount to it; and how each figure is built. A peer without US GAAP filings at the SEC shows as n/a with the reason, and unknown debt leaves EV unknown. Registers every filing and price it used and lists their IDs.

get_trading_snapshot: Takes ticker and sector_etf (optional, e.g. XLK). Returns what get_price_history gives over a year, then the trading context: average daily volume over 20 days and 3 months in shares and in the trading currency, the 50- and 200-day moving averages and the price against each, where the price sits in its 52-week range, and the 1-, 3-, 6- and 12-month returns against the S&P 500 and the sector ETF, from Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint. Registers the prices as sources (market data) and lists their IDs.

get_market_regime: Takes no input. Returns the market regime, each figure with its as-of time: the S&P 500 and Nasdaq (level, year to date, distance from the 52-week high and low), the VIX and its 1-year percentile, 3-month, 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields and the 2s10s curve, investment-grade and high-yield credit spreads, the fed funds target, the dollar index and USD/CNY, WTI, gold and copper, and the eleven sector ETFs' 1- and 3-month strength against the S&P 500, from Yahoo Finance and FRED; a figure that fails to load is named as not available. Registers each series as a source (market data) and lists their IDs.

get_insider_activity: Takes ticker and months (1-24, default 6). Returns insider transactions from the company's Form 4 filings over that window: purchases against sales by count, shares and value, with 10b5-1 plan sales and tax-withholding sales apart, the other transaction codes, the insiders with the largest purchases and sales, and a row per filing and code; it reads at most the 40 most recent filings and says so when the window holds more. Registers EDGAR's Form 4 list and the filings with the largest trades as primary sources and lists their IDs.

search_public_records: Takes source (openfda_device, openfda_drug, openfda_recalls, clinicaltrials, nhtsa_recalls, usaspending or sec_fulltext), query and limit (records a list, default 10, at most 20 in all). Returns the matching regulator and government records (FDA 510(k) clearances, De Novo grants and PMA approvals; Drugs@FDA applications; device recalls and drug enforcement reports; ClinicalTrials.gov studies; NHTSA vehicle recalls; federal contracts, grants and loans by recipient name; EDGAR full-text search of the last five years), each with its ID, title, date, status and link. Registers each record as a primary source and lists their IDs. Only SEC requests carry the contact User-Agent the SEC asks for; the others carry a generic one.

search_literature: Takes source (pubmed or arxiv), query and limit (default 10, at most 20). Returns the matching papers, each with its ID, title, year, authors, venue and link (PubMed adds the total count, journal and DOI; arXiv preprints are not peer reviewed). Registers each paper as an independent source (PubMed high confidence, arXiv medium) and lists their IDs.

read_playbook: Takes name (a playbook file, e.g. company_deep_dive_playbook.md), heading (optional) and page (default 1). Returns the investment-analysis skill's playbook text under that heading, up to the next heading of the same or a higher level, 11,000 characters a page; with no heading, the whole file if it fits, otherwise its list of headings with their sizes. Returns an error listing the playbooks, or the file's headings, when the name or heading does not match.

read_client_document: Takes name (a document attached to the request) and page (default 1). Returns one page of the document's text, about 9,000 characters, headed by its confidentiality label, its format, what the page covers and how to cite it (e.g. "deck.pdf p.N"); each block is marked with its page, slide or sheet, and text a reader would not see (white, tiny or hidden text, hidden slides, sheets, rows or columns, speaker notes, comments, document properties) is kept and labelled. Reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, Markdown and text; a file with no readable text comes back marked "[needs OCR or a text version]". If no attached document has that name, returns an error listing the attached names.

register_source: Takes title, source_type (primary, near-primary, independent, company-provided, market data or OCR; common descriptions such as "primary filing" or "independent media" are mapped to these), location (the URL retrieved, or the client document's name and page, which becomes its path under the delivery folder's sources/), date, confidence (high, medium or low), claims (what it supports) and claims_weakened (optional, what it weakens). Adds the source to the case's register, credited to the caller, and returns its ID (S1, S2, ...), citation form and normalized type; returns the existing ID when the location is already registered.

list_sources: Takes page (default 1). Returns the case's source register as a table of ID, title, type, location, date and confidence, 40 sources a page.

get_case_status: Takes no input. Returns what has arrived in the case: the run ID and scope (with the report's language), the client documents with their confidentiality labels, the number of registered sources, which workflow records are saved, each analyst's saved and missing sections and evidence blocks, each section's latest revision (owner, author, length, whether its check passed), the sections each owner has yet to save, the open rewrite items by owner, the number of user data requests, the latest version (rating, sections changed and missing, whether its check passed), the latest review (round, verdict, whether it is finished, items by severity, the revisions it requests by owner and which have been saved since), the review rounds left and the delivered versions.

set_case_scope: Takes company, ticker (empty for a private company), company_type (listed or private), decision (what the report serves) and language (en or zh; empty follows the request). Records the case's scope, which sets the report's cover, language and whether each section is held to the listed or the private standard, and returns it; a later change of company, ticker or type is recorded.

request_user_data: Takes database, query (what to look up), why (what it changes in the decision), how (how the user can get it) and section. Records a user data request, credited to the caller, for evidence behind a login, in a restricted registry or in a document the server cannot read; the requests appear in the rendered report's appendix and go to the user with the delivered report. Returns its ID (U1, U2, ...).

save_evidence_block: Takes section (a section key or number), queries (the searches and data-tool calls run for it), rows (the Deep Evidence Table: source IDs, source type, finding, mechanism, benchmark, what weakens it, investor implication, confidence and follow-up, each an object), gate_done (the numbers of the nine section-gate items met) and plan (the section plan: decision question, thesis sentence, why the section differs, mechanism, comparison set, counter-evidence, best visual and prohibited phrasing). Saves the block, credited to the caller, and returns whether it passes the skill's pre-draft gate and what it lacks; refuses the executive summary, which needs none.

get_evidence_matrix: Takes sections_wanted (optional section keys). Returns those sections' evidence blocks in full, or, with none named, a table of every section with its owner, who saved its evidence, the number of queries and rows, and whether its gate passed. Asks for fewer sections when they do not fit in one result.

get_gate: Takes sections_wanted (optional). Returns the skill's pre-draft gate for those sections (by default the caller's own; every section for the Lead and the IC Reviewer): whether each section's evidence block passes and what it lacks, then the source register's check (8 usable sources, 4 independent, 1 primary) and the research plan and profile requirements. It reports and never blocks.

save_research_plan: Takes spines (1-3, each with the question it must answer), industry_map, geo_lens, policy_lens, regulatory_databases, gates_done (the numbers of the 13 research-plan gates met) and hypothesis. Saves the case's research plan and returns its check and the list of gates.

save_profile_requirements: Takes items_done (the numbers of the profile items met) and evidence (what was done for each, with source IDs). Saves the profile requirements and returns their check and the list of items.

save_thesis_table: Takes rows (each with component, belief, evidence, counter_evidence, confidence and change). Saves the thesis table and returns its check, which includes the public-equity rows a listed company needs.

save_scenario_analysis: Takes scenarios (Bull, Base and Bear, each with probability, driver, evidence, outcome and implication), assumptions (3-5, each with confidence, what would disprove it, impact if wrong and early warning), consensus (the consensus, our view, the evidence and the event that would prove it), one_question (the question and why it decides the case), and optional analogs, capital_efficiency and position_sizing. Saves the scenario analysis and returns its check.

save_red_team_review: Takes answers (one for each of the skill's seven red-team questions) and weakest_section. Saves the red-team review, credited to the caller, and returns its check (every question answered, 600 characters in all).

get_workflow_doc: Takes kind (research_plan, profile_requirements, thesis_table, scenario_analysis, red_team_review, rewrite_plan, iteration_log or user_data_requests). Returns that record with its author.

save_memo_part: Takes section (a section key or number) and markdown (the section with inline [S#] citations and its components, each fenced by its name such as "::: kpi", without its title). Saves a new revision of that section, credited to the caller, and returns its revision number, length, owner, who saved it, and its check: rule findings (N1-N3, N6), the depth standard ported from the skill's check_depth.py (length, paragraphs, figures, source mentions and IDs, inline tags, coverage, granularity, components and tables) or, for the executive summary, check_report.py's briefing rules, any component the renderer cannot read, and whether a passing evidence block came first. Returns an error for an unknown section or one over 9,000 characters.

get_memo_parts: Takes sections_wanted (section keys or numbers; empty for a list) and version (a saved version's number, 0 for the latest, or empty for the sections as saved now). Returns the named sections whole, each headed by its revision, author and length, or, with none named, a table of every section with its number, owner, revision, length and check. Returns an error asking for fewer sections when they add up to more than 11,000 characters.

run_qa_loop: Takes no input. Renders the sections as saved now with the skill's template and runs every check (the rules, depth, report completeness, citations, the evidence matrix, the source register and the workflow records); when it fails, writes the rewrite plan, one item per failing section for its owner and one per report-level problem for the Lead, and logs the iteration. Returns the check, the open rewrite items by owner, the composite score and the skill's required fixes.

save_memo_version: Takes rating (Buy, Add, Hold, Watchlist, Hold off, Reduce, Sell or Avoid for a listed company; Invest now, Invest with conditions, Wait for milestone or Pass for a private one), data_as_of (YYYY-MM-DD) and the cover: tagline, business_summary, industry, headquarters and stage. Snapshots the latest revision of every section as a new version that never changes, renders it with the skill's template to HTML (with the source appendix, the user data requests, the areas for further diligence and the composite score worked out from section 14's weighted score table), prints the PDF, runs every check, and returns the version number, composite score, sections changed and missing, the files and the check; returns an error for a rating outside the company type's scale.

get_memo_check: Takes version (a saved version's number, 0 for the latest, or empty for the sections as saved now) and section (optional). Returns one section's check, a saved version's check as recorded when it was saved, or the whole report's check on the sections as saved now. It records nothing and never blocks.

start_review: Takes memo_version. Opens the IC Reviewer's review of that version (or reopens its earlier review of it, keeping the round) and returns the review ID, round, the sections changed since the previous review and the five passes.

save_review_pass: Takes review_id, pass_number (1 completeness sweep, 2 claim verification, 3 missing competitors, 4 product/market fit, 5 number stress-test), items (each with severity Critical, Important or Minor, sections, issue, action and an optional evidence_request) and notes. Saves that pass, replacing an earlier save of it, routes each item to its sections' owners, and returns the passes done and left; refuses a finished review.

finish_review: Takes review_id, verdict (approve, revise or reject), grade (A-F), top_three and ready. Closes the review after all five passes; approve needs no Critical item, and revise or reject needs at least one Critical or Important item. Records the revisions and evidence requested from each owner and returns the counts by severity and whom it asks.

get_latest_review: Takes no input. Returns the most recent review: its version and round, verdict, grade, top three fixes and readiness, then its items grouped by the owner who should fix them, with severity, pass, sections, issue, fix and evidence request, and each pass's notes; or that a review is still in progress.

deliver_memo: Takes version (default 0, the latest). Writes the delivery folder (sources/ with the client's documents, the HTML report and its PDF) and the report's Markdown, runs the skill's final check (report completeness, depth, citations and the folder's contract) and the rules, and records the delivery with the review gate for that exact version. Returns the review gate, the final check, the rule findings and the folder. It reports and never blocks.
