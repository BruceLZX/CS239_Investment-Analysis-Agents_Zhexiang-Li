message_bot: Rakazo built-in. Takes confirm_name (the recipient bot's name: "Fundamentals Analyst", "Market Analyst" or "IC Reviewer"), intent ("request") and message (the text to send, under 8,000 characters). Returns a receipt with ok, the recipient's bot id and name; the recipient's final reply arrives later as a new message to the sender.

web_search: Rakazo built-in (DuckDuckGo by default). Takes query and maxResults. Returns a list of results, each with title, url and snippet.

web_fetch: Rakazo built-in. Takes url and maxChars. Returns the page's url, title and text, cut to maxChars. Rakazo holds the fetch for approval when the URL did not come from the bot's own search results; filings are read with read_sec_filing instead.

find_sec_company: Takes ticker. Returns the company's ticker, 10-digit SEC CIK and registered name, from SEC EDGAR.

get_sec_financials: Takes ticker and years (1-5, default 3). Returns about 28 income statement, cash flow and balance sheet lines from the company's 10-K XBRL filings by fiscal year, with growth, margins, free cash flow, debt (naming the concepts it adds) and net cash worked out, and a table of the latest quarter, the same quarter a year earlier, year to date and trailing twelve months from its 10-Q filings. Registers the 10-Ks and the 10-Q it used as sources, credited to the caller, and lists their IDs and locations to cite.

list_sec_filings: Takes ticker, forms (default 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and DEF 14A) and limit (default 8). Returns the company's recent SEC filings, each with its form, filing date, report period, accession number and link.

read_sec_filing: Takes ticker, form (10-K or 10-Q, default 10-K), item (default 7; 10-K items 1, 1A, 7, 7A and 8, 10-Q items 1, 2 and 1A), page (default 1), keywords (optional) and accession (optional, for a filing other than the latest). Returns that item of the filing as text, about 9,000 characters a page and skipping the table of contents, or only the paragraphs that mention any keyword, searched on the MCP server, within the item or, with item empty, across the whole filing. Registers the filing as a source and lists its ID; returns an error listing the filing's items when the item is not found.

get_price_history: Takes ticker and period (1mo, 3mo, 6mo, 1y, 2y or 5y; default 1y). Returns the exchange and currency, the latest price with its UTC timestamp, the low, high, first and last daily close over the period with the change in percent, the 52-week range, the change to the latest price over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and the market backdrop (S&P 500, VIX and 10-year Treasury yield), from Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint. Registers the price data as sources (medium confidence) and lists their IDs.

get_valuation_snapshot: Takes ticker and peers (up to 4 tickers). Returns, for the company and each peer, the price with its time, market cap, enterprise value and trailing-twelve-month P/E, EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield and P/B, worked out from SEC XBRL filings and Yahoo prices; the debt counted in each EV and the concepts it adds; the company's P/E and EV/Sales at its last five fiscal year ends; the peer median and the company's premium or discount to it; and how each figure is built. A peer without US GAAP filings at the SEC shows as n/a with the reason, and unknown debt leaves EV unknown. Registers every filing and price it used and lists their IDs.

read_client_document: Takes name (a document attached to the request). Returns the document's text headed by its confidentiality label; if no attached document has that name, returns an error listing the attached names.

register_source: Takes title, source_type (primary filing, company website, market data, company-provided, independent media, industry data or other), location (the URL retrieved, or the client document's name), date, confidence (high, medium or low) and claims (what the source supports). Adds the source to the case's register, credited to the caller, and returns its ID (S1, S2, ...) and citation form; returns the existing ID when the location is already registered.

list_sources: Takes page (default 1). Returns the case's source register as a table of ID, title, type, location, date and confidence, 40 sources a page.

get_case_status: Takes no input. Returns what has arrived in the case: the run ID and scope, the attached client documents with their confidentiality labels, the number of registered sources, the evidence packets with the analyst that saved each and the parts they support, each analyst's first-round packets, each memo part's latest revision (owner, author, length and whether its check passed), the parts each owner has yet to save, the latest version (rating, parts changed, whether its check passed), the latest review (round, verdict, must-fix count, the revisions it requests by owner and which have been saved since, the analysts it asks for evidence and their follow-up packets so far), the review rounds left, and the delivered versions.

set_case_scope: Takes company, ticker (empty for a private company), company_type (listed or private) and decision (what the memo serves). Records the case's scope, which sets the memo's header and whether each part is checked to the listed or the private standard, and returns it; a later change of company, ticker or type is recorded.

save_evidence_packet: Takes topic, summary, findings (each ending with its source IDs), source_ids, counter_evidence and gaps, and optionally parts (the memo parts it supports), risks, scenario_drivers and searches (the queries and filing reads run, with the part each served). Saves the packet to the case file, credited to the analyst whose endpoint made the call; a packet saved after a review is marked as the follow-up for that review. Returns the packet's ID (E1, E2, ...), the analyst, the review it follows up (if any) and any cited source IDs that are not in the register.

get_evidence_packets: Takes packet_ids (optional). Returns the saved evidence packets, all of them or those named, with their analyst, the review they follow up (if any), the parts they support, summary, findings, risks, scenario drivers, counter-evidence and gaps; when they do not fit in one result, returns a list of the packets and their sizes to read a few at a time.

save_memo_part: Takes part (executive_summary, business, financials, valuation, competition, risks, scenarios, one_question or takeaways) and markdown (the part with inline [S#] citations, without its heading). Saves a new revision of that part, credited to the caller, and returns its revision number, length, owner, who saved it, and its check: rule findings (N1-N3, N6) and the part's depth standard (length, figures, source IDs, tables, coverage, evidence gate). Returns an error for an unknown part or a part over 9,000 characters.

get_memo_parts: Takes parts (part keys, or "sources" for the generated Sources table; empty for a list) and version (a saved version's number, 0 for the latest, or empty for the parts as saved now). Returns the named parts whole, each headed by its revision, author and length, or, with no parts named, a table of every part with its owner, revision, length and check. Returns an error asking for fewer parts when they add up to more than 11,500 characters.

save_memo_version: Takes rating (Buy, Add, Hold, Reduce, Sell, Watchlist or Hold-off for a listed company; Invest, Wait or Pass for a private one) and data_as_of (YYYY-MM-DD). Snapshots the latest revision of every part as a new version that never changes, generates the header, the Sources part (the cited sources only) and the disclaimer, and returns the version number, the parts changed since the last version, the missing parts and the whole memo's check; returns an error for any other rating.

get_memo_check: Takes version (a saved version's number, 0 for the latest, or empty for the parts as saved now) and part (optional). Returns one part's check, or the whole memo's: rule findings (cited IDs missing from the register, numbers without a source, company claims without a verification status, a rating the evidence gate does not support, internal working material), each part's depth problems, and problems across parts (missing parts, a summary that does not state the rating, sentences repeated across parts). It reports and never blocks.

save_review: Takes memo_version, verdict (approve, revise or reject), must_fix (items, each with part: a part key or "memo"; issue; fix; and evidence_from: "fundamentals", "market" or empty) and should_fix (a list of suggestions). Saves the review with its round, routes each item to its part's owner and each evidence request to the analyst it names, and returns the review's ID (R1, R2, ...), round, the owners asked for revisions and the analysts asked for evidence; returns an error for an unknown verdict, part or analyst, or a memo version that does not exist.

get_latest_review: Takes no input. Returns the most recent review: the memo version and round it covers, its verdict, its must-fix items grouped by the owner who should fix them, its should-fix items and its evidence requests by analyst ("none" when there are none).

deliver_memo: Takes version (0 for the latest). Records the delivery, writes the version's assembled memo to memo_v<N>.md in the run folder, and returns the delivered version, the review gate (whether that version was reviewed and approved, and by which review) and the memo check's findings. It never blocks a delivery.
